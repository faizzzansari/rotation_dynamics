Basics of Circular Motion

Kya gol ghoomna itna aasan hai?

Core Concept

Jab koi bhi cheez ek fixed point (centre) ke aage-peeche gol ghoomti hai, use Circular Motion kehte hain.

Socho aap straight road pe bhag rahe ho - waha aap distance (m) cover karte ho aur aapki speed (m/s) hoti hai.
Par jab aap gol (circle) mein bhagte ho, to aap angle ($ \theta $) cover karte ho! Isliye gol ghoomne mein hum Angular words use karte hain.

Visual Concept: Circular Motion

Notice karo: Velocity hamesha circle ke tangent (bahar nikalne ki koshish) mein hoti hai.

Dictionary (Naye Words)
1. Uniform Circular Motion (UCM)

Jab aapki cycle ke pahiye ki speed fixed (constant) ho. Speed wahi hai, lekin direction har second badal rahi hai (gol ghoomne ke liye).
Kyunki direction badal rahi hai, iska matlab velocity badal rahi hai. Aur jaha velocity badalti hai, waha acceleration hota hai! (UCM is an accelerated motion).

2. Non-Uniform Circular Motion

Jab aap fan ON karte ho, uski speed dhire-dhire badhti hai (gol ghoomte hue). Ye non-UCM hai.

Real-Life Connect

Ek pathar (stone) ko rassi (string) se bandho aur hawa mein gol ghumao.
Agar aap usko ek hi speed se ghumate rahoge, to wo UCM hai.
Agar rassi achanak toot jaye, to pathar gol nahi ghoomega! Wo seedha usi disha (direction) mein bhaagega jahan wo us second tha (Tangent ki line mein). Yahi velocity $v$ ki direction hoti hai.

Important Formulas

Straight line motion vs Circular motion

Speed = Distance / Time
Linear: $ v = \frac{s}{t} $
Angular Speed = Angle / Time
Angular: $ \omega = \frac{\theta}{t} $

(Omega $\omega$ is angular speed)

Relation between straight speed (v) and angular speed ($\omega$): $ v = r \times \omega $

Dhyan rakho: 'r' yaha radius hai circle ka.

Step-by-Step Example (Exam Level)
Question: Ek cycle ka pahiya 120 RPM (Rotations Per Minute) se ghoom raha hai. Uski angular velocity ($\omega$) rad/s mein batao.

Teacher (Aftab Sir) ka Dimaag (How to think):

1

Pehle dekho diya kya hai: Frequency (chakkars) di hai minute mein. Ise $n$ bolte hain.
$ n = 120 $ RPM (Rotations Per Minute)

2

Physics mein humein time hamesha Seconds mein chahiye hota hai.
So, 1 minute mein 60 seconds hote hain. Pahiye ke 1 second ke chakkar nikalte hain:
$ n = \frac{120}{60} $ rotations per second (rps/Hz)
$ n = 2 $ Hz (Yani 1 second mein 2 chakkar)

3

Humein Angular Velocity ($\omega$) nikalni hai. Ek pura chakkar (circle) angle mein kitna hota hai? $360^\circ$ ya $2\pi$ radians.
Agar 1 chakkar = $2\pi$ angle,
To $n$ chakkar = $2\pi \times n$ angle.
Formula ban gaya: $ \omega = 2\pi n $

Ab bas value daalo:
$ \omega = 2 \times \pi \times 2 $
$ \omega = 4\pi $ rad/s. Ye raha final answer!

Sabse Badi Galti (Don't do this!)

Bacche direct 120 value utha kar $\omega = 2\pi(120)$ kar dete hain. MAHA-GALTI!
Hamesha RPM (Minute) ko $60$ se divide karke rps (Seconds) mein badlo. Physics ka rule number 1: Units S.I. honi chahiye (Yani time Seconds mein).

Khud Check Karo

Agar fan 60 RPM se ghoom raha hai, to $\omega$ kya hoga?

Tap to see Answer
1. RPM ko rps banao: $n = 60/60 = 1$ rps.
2. Formula lagao: $\omega = 2\pi n$
3. Ans: $\omega = 2\pi(1) = 2\pi$ rad/s.

Forces in Circular Motion

Centripetal aur Centrifugal ka Khel

Core Concept

Newton baba ne kaha tha: "Bina force ke koi cheez apni direction nahi badal sakti".
Circular motion mein direction har second badal rahi hai. Iska matlab ek Force lagatar chahiye jo usko straight jane se roke aur circle mein kheench ke rakhe.
Is centre ki taraf kheenchne wale force ko Centripetal Force (CPF) kehte hain. Bina iske gol ghoomna IMPOSSIBLE hai.

Visual Concept: Real vs Fake Force

Outside View (Real Force)

Zameen par khade insaan ko dikhta hai ki Force centre ki taraf (andar) lag raha hai.

Inside View (Fake Force)

Object par baithe insaan ko lagta hai use koi bahar ki taraf fenk raha hai.

Centripetal Force (CPF)

  • Direction: Hamesha Centre ki taraf (Inward).
  • Reality: Ye ek REAL force hai. Koi na koi isko produce karta hai (jaise rassi ka Tension, ya zameen ka Friction).
  • Formula: $ F_c = \frac{mv^2}{r} = mr\omega^2 $

Centrifugal Force (CFF)

  • Direction: Hamesha Centre se door (Outward).
  • Reality: Ye ek FAKE (Pseudo) force hai. Koi ise laga nahi raha, bas Newton ka 1st law (Inertia) ki wajah se feel hota hai.
  • Formula: Magnitude same hai: $ \frac{mv^2}{r} $
Real-Life Connect

Aap ek car mein baithe ho. Driver achanak gaadi ko left turn marta hai (gol ghumata hai).
Kya hota hai? Aap right side (bahar ki taraf) window pe jaake takrate ho!

1. Bahar Khada Insaan kahega: "Car left ghoomi (Car par Centripetal force laga tire aur road ke friction se). Par andar baithe insaan par force nahi laga, wo seedha jana chahta tha (Inertia), isliye window se takraya."

2. Andar Baitha Insaan kahega: "Main aaram se baitha tha, pata nahi kis adrishya (invisible) taqat ne mujhe bahar ki taraf dhakka de diya!" (Isi ko wo Centrifugal Force naam dega).
"Darr Mat Lo!" (Concept Clarity)

Bacche sochte hain ki CPF aur CFF dono ek sath ek hi object par lagte hain aur ek doosre ko cancel kar dete hain. Ye galat hai!

Dono kabhi ek sath use nahi hote. Agar aap zameen (Inertial frame) se math solve kar rahe ho, to sirf Centripetal (CPF) lo.
Agar aap ghoomti hui gaadi ya jhule ke andar (Non-inertial frame) baith kar equation bana rahe ho, tab aap equation balance karne ke liye Centrifugal (CFF) add karte ho.

Applications: Gaadi aur Road

Turning on curves without skidding

Application 1: Vehicle on Unbanked (Flat) Road

Jab koi car flat road (jo tedhi nahi hai) pe turn leti hai, to car ko circle mein ghoomne ke liye Centripetal Force koun deta hai? Sirf aur sirf tires aur road ke beech ka Static Friction ($f_s$).

Free Body Diagram

Step-by-Step Derivation (Very Easy):

  1. Upar ka force = Niche ka force (Car hawa mein nahi ud rahi). So, $ N = mg $
  2. Andar ka force = Centripetal force. Andar sirf friction lag raha hai. So, $ f_s = \frac{mv^2}{r} $
  3. Humein pata hai maximum friction kitna ho sakta hai? $ f_{s(max)} = \mu_s N $ (Jaha $\mu_s$ friction coefficient hai).
  4. Value daalo: $ \frac{mv_{max}^2}{r} = \mu_s (mg) $
  5. Dono side se mass '$m$' cancel ho gaya (Yani car ho ya bhari truck, limit dono ke liye same hai!).
    $ \frac{v_{max}^2}{r} = \mu_s g $
Maximum Safe Speed limit $ v_{max} = \sqrt{\mu_s r g} $
Application 2: Well of Death (Maut Ka Kuaa)

Yahan ek vertical cylinder (diwar) ke andar motorcycle wala gol ghoomta hai aur niche nahi girta! Kyun? Kyunki Friction use niche girne se rok raha hai, aur diwar se lagne wala Normal reaction use centre ki taraf (CPF) force de raha hai.

Free Body Diagram

Step-by-Step Derivation (Compare with Flat road):

  1. Upar ka force = Niche ka force (Taki wo niche na gire). So yaha: $ f_s = mg $
  2. Andar ka force = Centripetal force. Andar sirf Normal diwar dhakka de rahi hai. So, $ N = \frac{mv^2}{r} $
  3. Hum jante hain $ f_s \le \mu_s N $ hona chahiye rukne ke liye. Diwar par chipke rehne ke liye ek minimum speed chahiye, warna girenge.
  4. Value daalo: $ mg \le \mu_s \left(\frac{mv^2}{r}\right) $
  5. Mass '$m$' phir se cancel.
    $ g \le \frac{\mu_s v^2}{r} \implies v^2 \ge \frac{rg}{\mu_s} $
Minimum Safe Speed (girne se bachne ke liye) $ v_{min} = \sqrt{\frac{rg}{\mu_s}} $
Common Mistake Alert!

Flat road mein Normal upar hota hai ($N=mg$) aur friction centre ki taraf ($f_s = \frac{mv^2}{r}$).
Well of Death mein sab ULTA ho jata hai! Normal centre ki taraf ($N = \frac{mv^2}{r}$) aur friction upar ($f_s=mg$). Isko dhyan rakhna, exam mein mix mat karna.

Application 3: Banked Road (Slightly tilted road)

Barish ke mausam mein Flat road par sirf friction pe depend karna khatarnak hai (gaadi fisal sakti hai). Isliye engineer curve par road ko thoda ek side se utha dete hain (angle $\theta$ par tilt kar dete hain). Ise Banking of Road kehte hain. Isse Normal reaction ka ek hissa gaadi ko centre ki taraf dhakka deta hai (CPF ban jata hai)!

Components on Banked Road

Step-by-Step Derivation (Ignoring Friction for "Most Safe Speed"):

Hum maan rahe hain road poori chikni (frictionless) hai, fir bhi car turn le legi road tilt hone ki wajah se.

  1. Normal ($N$) ke do tukde (components) kiye. Ek upar gaya: $N \cos\theta$. Ek andar (centre ki taraf) gaya: $N \sin\theta$.
  2. Upar wala niche wale weight ko balance karega:
    $ N \cos\theta = mg $ --- (Equation 1)
  3. Andar wala component circle mein ghoomne ke liye CPF dega:
    $ N \sin\theta = \frac{mv^2}{r} $ --- (Equation 2)
  4. Equation 2 ko Equation 1 se divide kar do:
    $ \frac{N \sin\theta}{N \cos\theta} = \frac{mv^2 / r}{mg} $
  5. N aur m cut gaye. $ \sin/\cos $ ban gaya $ \tan $.
    $ \tan\theta = \frac{v^2}{rg} $
Most Optimum/Safe Speed ($v_s$) $ v_s = \sqrt{rg \tan\theta} $

Notice: Mass ($m$) gayab ho gaya. Yani ek truck aur cycle dono ke liye yeh 'safest speed' exactly same hogi usi road par!

Agar gaadi dhime (slow) jaye ($v < v_s$):

Gaadi niche phisalna chahegi, toh friction Upar (Up the slope) lagega use rokne ke liye.

$v_{min} = \sqrt{rg\left(\frac{\tan\theta - \mu_s}{1 + \mu_s \tan\theta}\right)}$
Agar gaadi bahot tez bhage ($v > v_s$):

Gaadi bahar (upar) fekayegi, toh friction Niche (Down the slope) lagega andar kheenchne ke liye.

$v_{max} = \sqrt{rg\left(\frac{\tan\theta + \mu_s}{1 - \mu_s \tan\theta}\right)}$

Conical Pendulum

Dhage se bandha pathar gol ghumana

Core Concept

Agar ek pathar (bob) ko dhage se bandh kar hawa mein horizontal circle mein ghumao, to dhaga hawa mein ek 'Cone' jaisa shape banata hai. Yaha dhage ki Tension ($T_0$) ka ek component pathar ko CPF deta hai.

Step-by-Step Derivation (Time Period):

  1. Tension ($T_0$) ke components liye. Upar gaya: $T_0 \cos\theta$. Andar gaya: $T_0 \sin\theta$.
  2. Upar aur Niche balance:
    $ T_0 \cos\theta = mg $ --- (1)
  3. Andar wala (CPF) de raha hai:
    $ T_0 \sin\theta = mr\omega^2 $ --- (2)
  4. (2) ko (1) se divide karo: $\frac{T_0 \sin\theta}{T_0 \cos\theta} = \frac{mr\omega^2}{mg} \implies \tan\theta = \frac{r\omega^2}{g}$
  5. Geometry se dekho, circle ka radius $r = L \sin\theta$ hota hai. Isko daalo aur $\omega$ nikalo.
  6. Humein Time period ($T$) chahiye. Formula hai: $T = \frac{2\pi}{\omega}$. Final equation banti hai:
Time Period of Conical Pendulum $ T = 2\pi\sqrt{\frac{L \cos\theta}{g}} $
Logic Test

Sawal: Kya main pathar ko itna tez ghuma sakta hu ki dhaga bilkul horizontal ($90^\circ$ flat) ho jaye?

Aftab Sir ka Jawab: NAAA! Socho kyun? Agar dhaga horizontal ho gaya to angle $\theta = 90^\circ$.
Formula dekho: Upar wala component tha $T_0 \cos\theta$. Agar $\theta = 90^\circ$, to $\cos 90^\circ = 0$.
Yani upar khinchne wala force ZERO ho jayega! Phir $mg$ (gravity) ko koun sambhalega? Pathar turant niche gir jayega. Isliye dhaga KABHI exactly horizontal nahi ho sakta.

Vertical Circular Motion (VCM)

Gravity ke sath Khel (Non-UCM)

Core Concept

Jab pathar ko rassi se bandh ke 'Upar-Niche' (Vertical circle) mein ghumate hain, to gravity apna role play karti hai.
- Upar jate waqt: Speed kam hoti hai (gravity niche khinchti hai).
- Niche aate waqt: Speed badhti hai.
Isliye ye ek Non-Uniform Circular Motion hai.

Concept: Upar wali position pe itni speed honi chahiye ki rassi dhili (slack) na ho, warna pathar sidhe niche gir jayega, circle pura nahi hoga.

Case 1: Mass on a STRING

Rassi dhili ho sakti hai, isliye top pe ek minimum tension $T_{top} \ge 0$ chahiye.

Position Minimum Speed
Highest (Top) $ \sqrt{rg} $
Lowest (Bottom) $ \sqrt{5rg} $
Midway (Horizontal) $ \sqrt{3rg} $
Tension Difference (Bottom - Top) = $ 6mg $
Case 2: Mass on a Rigid ROD

Lakdi/Rod kabhi dhili nahi hoti. Isliye top pe agar speed ZERO bhi ho jaye to rod pathar ko niche nahi girne degi.

Position Minimum Speed
Highest (Top) $ 0 $
Lowest (Bottom) $ \sqrt{4rg} = 2\sqrt{rg} $
Midway (Horizontal) $ \sqrt{2rg} $
Tension Difference (Bottom - Top) = $ 6mg $

Moment of Inertia ($I$)

Rotational Mass

Core Concept

Line mein chalne ke liye jo kaam Mass ($m$) karta hai (Rukawat ya Inertia dena), Gol ghoomne ke liye wahi kaam Moment of Inertia ($I$) karta hai.

Par $I$ sirf mass pe depend nahi karta, ispe bhi depend karta hai ki mass Axis se kitni door bikhra (distributed) hai. Agar mass axis ke bahot door hai, to $I$ jyada hoga, ghumana muskhil hoga.

Discrete Particles (Alag-alag points) $ I = \sum m_i r_i^2 $
Continuous Body (Poori ek body) $ I = \int r^2 dm $

Radius of Gyration ($K$)

Agar ek poori badi body ko hata kar, uska saara Mass ek choti si gaali (point) banakar kisi doori '$K$' par rakh dein, taaki M.I. ($I$) change na ho. Us doori ko Radius of Gyration kehte hain.

$ I = MK^2 \implies K = \sqrt{\frac{I}{M}} $

The Two Super-Heroes (Theorems)

Parallel Axes Theorem

Kisi bhi aadi-tedhi axis pe $I$ nikalna ho, agar wo Center of Mass se jane wali axis ke parallel hai.

$ I_O = I_C + Mh^2 $
  • $I_O$: Target axis ka M.I.
  • $I_C$: Center of mass wali axis ka M.I.
  • $h$: Dono parallel line ke beech ka distance.
Perpendicular Axes Theorem

Warning: Valid ONLY for 2D (Flat/Laminar) objects!

$ I_Z = I_X + I_Y $
  • Object X-Y plane mein rakha hona chahiye.
  • Teeno lines (X, Y, Z) ek hi point pe meet karni chahiye.

Rolling Motion

Ghoomna + Aage Badhna

Total Energy of Rolling Body

Pure rolling ka matlab wheel apne jagah pe ghoom bhi raha hai (Rotation) aur sath mein aage bhi badh raha hai (Translation). To uske paas 2 tarah ki Kinetic Energy hoti hai!

Total K.E. = Translational K.E. + Rotational K.E. $ E = \frac{1}{2}Mv^2 \left[1 + \frac{K^2}{R^2}\right] $

Ye bracket wala term $\left[1 + \frac{K^2}{R^2}\right]$ bata deta hai ki object Ring hai, Disc hai ya Sphere hai.

Rolling down an Incline (Velocity at bottom)

$ v = \sqrt{\frac{2gh}{1 + \frac{K^2}{R^2}}} $

Acceleration down the Incline

$ a = \frac{g \sin\theta}{1 + \frac{K^2}{R^2}} $
Race - Kaun Jeetega?

Ek Ring aur ek Solid Sphere dono ko inclined plane se ek sath choda. Niche pehle kaun pahuchega?

Tap to reveal the winner!
Pehle wo pahuchega jiska Acceleration ($a$) jyada hoga.
Formula mein dekho, jiske liye niche wala bracket $\left(1 + \frac{K^2}{R^2}\right)$ chota hoga, uska $a$ jyada hoga.

- Ring ke liye $\frac{K^2}{R^2} = 1$ (Bracket = 2)
- Solid Sphere ke liye $\frac{K^2}{R^2} = \frac{2}{5} = 0.4$ (Bracket = 1.4)

Kyunki Sphere ka bracket chota hai, uska acceleration zyada hai. Winner: Solid Sphere!
